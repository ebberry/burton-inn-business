# Step 1: LLC and business license

## What I could and couldn't verify
- Washington trade names are registered through the DOR business license application,
  not a separate DBA form (third-party sources; confirm on dor.wa.gov).
- The LLC must exist with the Secretary of State before the DOR license can be filed in its name.
- Fees: DOR's new-business application handling fee is $50 per WAC 458-02-200 (from the
  October research). Third-party sources quote $10 for an existing business and $5 per trade name.
  Confirm the exact total in the DOR wizard before paying.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm the LLC is fully filed with the Secretary of State (not just a name reservation).
      Save the certificate of formation to `../` (to be moved into a future `entity/` folder).
- [ ] Decide and check the trade name ("The Burton Inn") in DOR Business Lookup.
- [ ] Start the application at the DOR Business Licensing Wizard (dor.wa.gov).
- [ ] Describe the business as short-term lodging (rooms rented under 30 nights).
- [ ] Register for sales tax and lodging taxes when the wizard asks. Airbnb and similar
      platforms collect most state and local lodging taxes, but the LLC still files returns.
- [ ] Unincorporated King County has no general city business license (October research).
- [ ] Save the license and UBI number in `../permits/` and note it in `../log.md`.
