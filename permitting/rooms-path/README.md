# Rooms-only path: 4 upstairs rooms as short-term rentals

Goal: first paying guest, with no kitchen, septic or liquor license involved.
Estimated 2 to 8 weeks if records show lodging has been used here before; add roughly
8 to 24 weeks if a change-of-occupancy review turns out to be needed.

Status starting point (2026-10-20): LLC started, nothing else done.

## The order that matters

Do not spend money on insurance, furnishings or listings until steps 1 to 3 come back.
The zoning answer and the lease are the two things that can stop this path.

## Decision points

- **Zoning letter says lodging is not allowed, or needs a resident operator:** stop and
  talk to King County about options before anything else. Record it in ../decisions.md.
- **Lease does not allow nightly lodging:** negotiate an amendment first.
- **Records show no lodging history:** expect a change-of-occupancy review; re-plan the timeline.
