# Step 4: Zoning letter

King County's Zoning Certification Letter verifies that a use is permitted under
Title 21A. A separate historical certification covers more comprehensive research on a
parcel's history and can take longer. You likely want the zoning certification, and the
historical version only if the permit records leave the occupancy history unclear.

## How to request
- Through King County DLS Permitting (MyBuildingPermit.com or the county's zoning
  inquiry process). I could not confirm which; call the Permitting Division at
  **206-296-6600** (the number on the county's zoning certification form) and ask.
- Fee: not confirmed. King County's fee page lists "Zoning Inquiry" under
  Pre-Development Services. The county's 2026 fees rose about 14% and most applications
  carry a $126 screening fee. Ask whether the letter does.
- Ask whether a **pre-application meeting** makes more sense. It lets you put the
  questions below to a planner directly, and the same meeting can cover Track B later.

## Questions to put in writing
Parcel: 192203-9032. Address: 24007 Vashon Hwy SW.

1. What is the zoning, and does it carry a P-suffix? Please provide the P-suffix text.
   (A 1980s Vashon plan ordinance mentions a P-condition near Burton limiting some
   parcels to "a guest inn".)
2. Is renting four guest rooms nightly (under 30 days) permitted at this site? Under which
   use in KCC 21A.08: hotel/motel, bed and breakfast guesthouse, or other?
3. If bed and breakfast guesthouse: is there a resident-operator requirement or a room limit?
4. If a use is allowed only as nonconforming: has it been discontinued long enough
   (KCC 21A.32.045) to lose that status? The inn has been closed roughly five years.
5. Is an STR registration or permit required in unincorporated King County for this use?
6. Parking required for the lodging use, and is the existing on-site parking adequate?
7. Is the building a designated landmark, or subject to any historic review?
8. Is a restaurant or cafe use, including coffee service, permitted at this site, and are there any conditions? The Assessor lists a 50-seat table-service restaurant and a bed and breakfast present use; please confirm what the county recognizes.
9. Anything else on the parcel (conditions, easements, overlays) affecting reopening?
