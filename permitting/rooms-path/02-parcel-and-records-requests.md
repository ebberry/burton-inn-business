## Records request (King County Permitting Records Center)
- Form: "Permitting Research Request" on King County's permit records research page.
- Send the completed form to **Permitrecords@kingcounty.gov**. Phone request line: 206-296-6696.
- Reviewing files is free; copies are charged at the standard county rate.
- Residential permit records go back to about 1970; some plans to 1987. This is a
  commercial building from 1991 or 1993, so it should be in range.
- The county's online request system is described as the fastest route; use it if it
  offers a commercial permit option.
