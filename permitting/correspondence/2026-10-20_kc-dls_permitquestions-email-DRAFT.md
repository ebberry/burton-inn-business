**Subject:** Allowed lodging and restaurant use, 24007 Vashon Hwy SW, parcel 192203-9032

Hello,

I am reopening a closed inn and restaurant at 24007 Vashon Hwy SW, Vashon (parcel 192203-9032,
zoned NB, unincorporated King County). The Assessor lists the present use as Bed & Breakfast and shows
four guest rooms with private baths and a 50-seat restaurant. The building was built in 1991 and has been
closed roughly five years. I would appreciate answers to the following, or a pointer to the right person.

1. How does the county classify the existing lodging use at this site? In NB, KCC 21A.08.050 does not
   list hotel/motel and allows a bed and breakfast guesthouse only under condition 15. Is the existing use
   recognized as a legally established use, conforming or nonconforming?
2. Does the site have a P-suffix or property-specific condition? A 1980s Vashon plan ordinance mentions a
   "guest inn" near Burton.
3. If the lodging is treated as a bed and breakfast guesthouse, does the "permanent residence of the
   operator" condition apply here? Could an owner or on-site manager living in the building satisfy it?
4. If the lodging is a nonconforming use, has it been discontinued long enough under KCC 21A.32.045 to
   lose that status, given the closure of about five years? What would the county accept as evidence?
5. Is the restaurant (eating and drinking place) permitted at this site, and can it serve the public while
   the upstairs rooms operate as lodging?
6. Does the permit history show the guest rooms were permitted for transient lodging, with what occupancy
   classification? Are there open code enforcement cases?
7. Is any short-term rental registration or permit required in unincorporated King County for nightly rooms?

I understand informal answers are not binding. If a written Zoning Certification Letter is the better route
for questions 1 to 4, please tell me which scope and fee tier applies.
