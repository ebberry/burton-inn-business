# What King County's zoning code says about lodging and restaurants in the NB zone

Source: King County Code Title 21A, section 21A.08 use tables (county's published Title 21A page,
read 2026-10-20). I parsed the table columns directly, but the code is amended often. Treat this as a
strong lead and confirm with King County staff in writing.

Parcel 192203-9032 is zoned **NB** (Neighborhood Business) per the Assessor.

## Findings for NB

| Use | NB column | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Hotel/Motel** (21A.08.050) | **Not allowed** (permitted only in CB, RB and O) | A "hotel" or "inn" classification for the rooms does not work in NB as of this code. |
| **Bed and breakfast guesthouse** (21A.08.050) | **Permitted, with conditions (note 15)** | See conditions below. |
| **Eating and drinking places** (21A.08.070) | **Permitted, excluding drinking places (note 10)** | A restaurant is allowed. A bar or tavern as the principal use (SIC 5813) is not. |

**Note 15, conditions on a bed and breakfast guesthouse in NB:**
1. Only as accessory use to the **permanent residence of the operator**.
2. **Served meals are limited to paying guests.**
3. No more than **five rooms accommodating up to ten guests**.

Definition (21A.06.090): a dwelling unit or accessory building within which bedrooms are available for paying guests.

## What this means for the Burton Inn

- The rooms path likely depends on how the county classifies the **existing** use at this site. Three possibilities:
  1. **B&B guesthouse.** Matches four rooms and a 10-guest cap, but needs an operator who lives on site, and meals only for guests.
  2. **Legal nonconforming use** (an inn use older than the current code). Status can be lost if the use stops for too long; the site has been closed roughly five years. See KCC 21A.32.045 and the October research.
  3. **A site-specific P-suffix condition** (a 1980s Vashon plan ordinance mentions a P-condition near Burton limiting some parcels to "a guest inn"). The Assessor shows no P-suffix, but the zoning letter or a county zoning lookup would.
- **A public restaurant plus a B&B at the same site** may not fit together under note 15 if the lodging is classified as a B&B guesthouse (meals only to paying guests). The restaurant on its own is allowed in NB. Ask how the county treats a restaurant and B&B combined.
- The 10-guest cap and four rooms fit the B&B limits. A 16-guest cap would not.
- **Resident-operator requirement** is now the key question (decision D2).
- Rural neighborhood centers also cap individual personal-service and retail establishments at 5,000 sq ft and combined at 15,000 sq ft; the building is 3,363 sq ft, so this is not a problem.
