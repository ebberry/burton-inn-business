# Walkthrough

The path to reopening, in order. Tick boxes as you go, and mirror status in the
workbook's Master List (`#` numbers below). Weeks are rough estimates from the
October 2026 research; see [research/2026-10_permitting-and-licensing-foundation.md](research/2026-10_permitting-and-licensing-foundation.md).

Two tracks run side by side:

- **Track A: Rooms only.** The 4 upstairs rooms as short-term rentals. No kitchen,
  septic or liquor license needed. Gate: the zoning letter and the lease.
- **Track B: Kitchen and bar.** The critical path is Public Health's full plan
  review (kitchen closed more than 365 days), tied to septic/grease and the Type I hood.
  The liquor license runs in parallel and is filed about 90 days before opening.

## Phase 0: Decide and gather (weeks 0 to 4)

Do these first. Nothing below should cost real money until the lease and zoning are clear.

**Decisions** (record outcomes in [decisions.md](decisions.md))
- [ ] D1. Guest cap: 10 total (likely R-3) or up to 16 (R-1)
- [ ] D2. Will an owner or manager live on site? (may matter for a B&B guesthouse)
- [ ] D3. Seat count and hours (assumed about 40 seats; 50+ occupants makes it A-2)
- [ ] D4. Alcohol path: Beer/Wine Restaurant first, or Spirits/Beer/Wine from day one
- [ ] D5. License structure: Burton Inn LLC as licensee, operator sublease, or management agreement
- [ ] D6. Target opening date (sets when to file with LCB)

**Lease and landlord** (J.T. Sheffield LLC)
- [ ] Lease use clause allows nightly lodging, restaurant and bar
- [ ] Written landlord consent for lodging and the liquor license
- [ ] Who pays for septic, grease interceptor and hood work
- [ ] Roof fan removal history

**Records requests** (#1; file copies in [records/](records/))
- [ ] Confirm the parcel number (LoopNet listed 24012, not 24007)
- [ ] KC permit history: 1993 permit, Certificate of Occupancy, occupancy class, expired permits, code enforcement
- [ ] PHSKC food permit history, inspections, last closure date
- [ ] PHSKC OSS (septic) as-built and design flow

**Zoning** (#2)

## Track B: Kitchen and bar (roughly 24 to 32 weeks)

### B1. Investigate (weeks 0 to 4)
- [ ] Equipment inventory: make, model, NSF/UL labels
- [ ] Septic condition inspection by a King County certified On-site System Maintainer (#4)
- [ ] Hood: find the UL listing; duct inspection

### B2. Design and submit (weeks 4 to 12)
- [ ] Septic and grease interceptor design by a licensed OSS designer (#5)
- [ ] Hood, upblast fan and makeup air design (#8)
- [ ] UL 300 suppression design (#9)
- [ ] Menu and risk level set (Risk 2 or 3)
- [ ] PHSKC full plan review submitted: site plan, equipment floor plan, equipment schedule, menu, questionnaire. $1,008 base (#7)

### B3. Permits issued (weeks 8 to 20)
- [ ] OSS / grease interceptor approval (#5)
- [ ] KC mechanical permit (#8)
- [ ] KC Fire Marshal suppression permit (#9)
- [ ] L&I electrical permit (#10)
- [ ] PHSKC plumbing permit (#11)
- [ ] KC building permit, only if work goes beyond repair (#12)
- [ ] Sign permit, optional (#20)

### B4. Build (weeks 12 to 28)
- [ ] Hood, fan, makeup air installed; NFPA 96 cleaning certificate
- [ ] Suppression installed with fuel shutoff and K-class extinguisher
- [ ] Sinks: handwash, 3-compartment, prep, mop; water heater capacity checked
- [ ] Grease interceptor installed, maintenance schedule filed

### B5. Liquor license (file about 90 days before target open) (#15)
- [ ] Apply through DOR Business Licensing Service
- [ ] Licensing specialist contact (about 1 week); Phase 1 (about 10 days)
- [ ] King County notified (20-day response window)
- [ ] 14-day public notice posted at the premises
- [ ] Submit: business structure, financing and funding sources, lease, floor plan
- [ ] Fingerprints and background checks for all true parties of interest
- [ ] Approval letter received (acts as a 30-day license)

### B6. Finals and open (weeks 24 to 32)
- [ ] Mechanical, fire and building finals; CO confirmed (#13)
- [ ] PHSKC preoperational inspection; operating permit posted (#14)
- [ ] Food worker cards for all food handlers; Certified Food Protection Manager (#17)
- [ ] MAST permits for every server (#16)
- [ ] Fire operational permits / annual inspection, if required (#19)
- [ ] Music licensing (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC), optional
- [ ] **Open**
